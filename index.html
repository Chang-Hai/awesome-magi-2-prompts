Test one movement and one sound

Start with a reference photo of a lamp you can use. A second image can show the switch. Keep the camera still so you can find the exact frame where the switch moves.

Frame the lamp from the reference photo on a dark desk. Keep the shade and switch in view.
0–2 seconds. Hold the shot steady with quiet room tone.
At 2 seconds, a hand moves the switch once. Play one click as the light turns on.
2–6 seconds. Hold the shot and keep the lamp’s shape, crop and room tone steady.

Find the first bright frame and the first audible click in your editor. Write down the time difference. You can run this exercise in a video generator that supports reference media and native sound, such as GetMagi2.
