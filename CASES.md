## 9×9 Sudoku Probe

**Published prompt** · MAGI-2 flagship pre-trained checkpoint · Research

**[▶ Watch Video](https://static.magi.world/media/website/3522a0a956303744982926dce8df03555765e7ff8c7693ba887a3e5d6e60a40a.mp4)** · [Original source](https://sand.ai/blog/magi-2#visual-reasoning) · **Credit** Sand.ai

7.57s · 480 × 480 · 16 fps · audio stream present

Sand.ai asked a pre-trained model, with prompt enhancement off, to complete a Sudoku grid on screen. This is a documented probe of one checkpoint.

**What to watch for**

- Pause on the starting and final boards to check that the original digits are retained.
- Check rows, columns and 3×3 boxes on the last board rather than relying on the drawing animation.

**Input prompt quoted from the original source**

```text
Solve this 9×9 Sudoku puzzle step by step, filling each empty cell one by one while keeping all given digits unchanged.
```
